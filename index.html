About

A table set with what's local

Yami Foody started as a small grill stand in Transmitter before it had a dining room to speak of. The idea never changed: fresh meat on the fire, bread and chips done right, and no long wait for it.

Every order comes off the grill to order — shawarma wrapped fresh, chicken grilled or fried, and burgers stacked the way you ask for them.
